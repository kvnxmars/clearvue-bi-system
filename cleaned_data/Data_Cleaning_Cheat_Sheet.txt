QUICK REFERENCE: Sales Data Categories
==================================================

MAIN SALES REPS:
02 = R       04 = BM      06 = DA
03 = BJ      05 = RL      07 = LA
01 = HEAD OFFICE

KEY CATEGORIES FOR ANALYSIS:
• SALES_REP           - Actual sales (use for performance)
• CROSS_TERRITORY_SALE - Sales outside normal territory
• CONSIGNMENT         - Products placed with customers
• SAMPLES             - Free samples (exclude from revenue)
• STOCK_MOVEMENT      - Internal transfers (exclude from revenue)

FILTERING TIPS:
For sales reports: Use REP_CATEGORY = 'SALES_REP'
For rep performance: Use SALES_REP_ID field
For territory analysis: Use TERRITORY field

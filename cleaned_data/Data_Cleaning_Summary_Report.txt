EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
We cleaned 67,988 sales transactions to make the data more useful for analysis.
The main improvement was categorizing sales representative codes to separate
actual sales from special transactions like consignments and samples.

KEY BENEFITS:
• Accurate sales performance measurement
• Clear separation of different business processes
• Better understanding of sales territories
• Cleaner data for reporting and dashboards

WHAT WE CLEANED
----------------------------------------
1. SALES REPRESENTATIVE CODES (REP_CODE)
   BEFORE: Mixed codes like '02JUL', 'CONS3', 'STOCK' all treated the same
   AFTER:  Organized into 13 clear business categories
   IMPACT: Now you can analyze actual sales vs. other transactions separately

2. DATES
   BEFORE: Dates included unnecessary time (00:00:00)
   AFTER:  Clean date format only
   IMPACT: Easier date analysis and reporting

3. SALES REP IDENTIFICATION
   BEFORE: Hard to identify which sales rep made each sale
   AFTER:  Clear sales rep IDs extracted (02, 03, 04, etc.)
   IMPACT: Easy sales performance tracking by rep

4. TERRITORY ANALYSIS
   BEFORE: Couldn't easily see cross-territory sales
   AFTER:  Territory codes extracted from rep codes
   IMPACT: Understand which territories are performing well

NEW BUSINESS CATEGORIES
----------------------------------------
We created these categories to separate different types of transactions:

 1. SALES_REP                 41704 records (61.3%)
     Regular sales by main reps (02=R, 03=BJ, 04=BM, etc.)
 2. SALES_REP_SPECIAL          2419 records (3.6%)
     Special transactions by sales reps
 3. CROSS_TERRITORY_SALE      15174 records (22.3%)
     Sales made outside rep's normal territory

     Consignment sales - products placed with customers

 7. STOCK_MOVEMENT               80 records (0.1%)
     Internal stock transfers between locations
 8. SAMPLES                      86 records (0.1%)
     Free samples given to customers

     Products sent for display stands
10. HOUSE_CONSIGNMENT            72 records (0.1%)
     Internal house consignments
11. REPAIRS                    1095 records (1.6%)
     Repair services (not product sales)
12. PROBLEM_ACCOUNT               1 records (0.0%)
     Accounts with payment issues

     Miscellaneous transactions
